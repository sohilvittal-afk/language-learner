Word Bank

Every word on the platform lives here. Practice and Side Quests both draw from this list. Each word is shown with its translation into your preferred language — change it on your Profile.

Add a word

Just type the word and pick its language — the AI fills in the definition, part of speech, example sentence, difficulty, and grammar forms (imperfectum/perfectum for verbs, article/plural for nouns, comparative/superlative for adjectives).

Or add words from an image

Upload a screenshot or photo — a page from a book, a vocabulary list, even a picture of objects. The AI reads it, pulls out the words in the language selected above, and you pick which ones to add.
